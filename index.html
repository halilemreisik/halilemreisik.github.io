About

I'm an undergraduate at ETH Zürich studying Mechanical Engineering, drawn to the point where mechanics, electronics and software meet.

Before Zürich I finished both a German Abitur and a Turkish Lise diploma in Istanbul, graduating as class valedictorian in mathematics and physics. That mix of rigour and curiosity still shapes how I work: understand the fundamentals, then build something real with them.

Outside the lecture halls I'm a photographer and videographer. I shoot, edit and produce, from alpine landscapes to short films, and I help run student life in Zürich. The engineering and the camera feed each other more than you'd think.
